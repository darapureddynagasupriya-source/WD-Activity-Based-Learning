About BTechFest 2026

TechFest is a three-day college festival that brings together students to explore creativity, technical skills, teamwork and talent.

Participate in coding challenges, robotics, dance battles, music shows, photography contests and expert-led workshops. Meet new people, learn new skills and celebrate campus life.

📋 Rules & Guidelines

  • Complete registration before the event deadline.
  • Participants must follow the event coordinator's instructions.
  • Maintain discipline and respect all participants.
  • Plagiarism and unfair practices are prohibited.
